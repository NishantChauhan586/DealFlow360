You are the senior frontend engineer and product designer responsible for building the frontend of **DealFlow360 — An Intelligent, Self-Governing Sales Operations Platform**.

The goal is NOT to create a generic admin dashboard.

Build a premium, modern enterprise SaaS experience inspired by the quality and interaction design of products such as Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Raycast, Notion, and award-winning Awwwards websites — while maintaining a unique visual identity for DealFlow360.

## PRODUCT

DealFlow360 manages the complete sales lifecycle:

QUOTE → RISK → RECOMMENDATION → APPROVAL → FULFILLMENT → NEGOTIATION → RE-APPROVAL → BILLING → DEAL HEALTH → CASH

The frontend must make this workflow visually understandable.

Core product capabilities:

- Quotation creation and management
- Multi-tier discount governance
- Automated approval routing
- Live margin calculation
- Upsell/cross-sell recommendations
- Multi-warehouse fulfillment
- Backorders
- One-time + recurring billing
- Customer quotation negotiation portal
- Deal health monitoring
- Anomaly alerts
- Reporting and analytics
- Audit trail
- Role-based experiences

## ANIMATION SYSTEM

Animations are important, but performance is more important.

Use **Motion / Framer Motion** for normal UI interactions:

- page transitions
- card entrance
- dropdowns
- dialogs
- expanding sections
- hover states
- list changes
- modal transitions
- number changes
- timeline transitions

Use **GSAP selectively** for premium interactions that benefit from timeline control.

Examples:

- dashboard hero/overview entrance
- complex quote workflow transitions
- approval visualization
- warehouse allocation visualization
- special data transitions

Do NOT use GSAP everywhere.

Animation principles:

- Fast
- Subtle
- Intentional
- Smooth
- Never block interaction

Typical durations:

- micro interaction: 120–180ms
- standard interaction: 180–250ms
- larger transition: 300–500ms

Prefer transform and opacity animations.

Respect:

prefers-reduced-motion
