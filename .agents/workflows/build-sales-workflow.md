description: Implement and visually connect the complete DealFlow360 sales lifecycle from quotation through billing and deal health.
---

Use the DealFlow360 lifecycle:

QUOTE
→ RISK
→ RECOMMENDATION
→ APPROVAL
→ FULFILLMENT
→ NEGOTIATION
→ RE-APPROVAL
→ BILLING
→ DEAL HEALTH

Every workflow screen should communicate:

Current state
Current owner
Risk
Required action
Next state

Important scenarios:

1. Sales representative creates a quotation.
2. Discount exceeds configured limits.
3. Risk engine identifies the violation.
4. Approval is automatically required.
5. Manager/Finance reviews the deal.
6. Approved deal moves to fulfillment.
7. Warehouse optimizer recommends allocation.
8. Customer receives the quotation through the separate customer portal.
9. Customer negotiates or requests changes.
10. Changes above thresholds trigger re-approval.
11. Confirmed deal proceeds to billing.
12. Deal health continuously monitors the lifecycle.

Do not bypass intermediate states merely to simplify the UI.
