Do not treat responsiveness as a final step.

Desktop should prioritize efficient enterprise workflows.

On smaller screens:
- adapt layouts
- collapse secondary information
- use drawers where appropriate
- allow controlled horizontal scrolling for complex tables
- maintain usable touch targets
