RULES = TRUTH

- discount limits
- approvals
- permissions
- risk thresholds
- fulfillment
- billing

AI may assist with:
- recommendations
- explanations
- deal summaries
- negotiation suggestions
- anomaly explanations
- follow-up suggestions
- upsell/cross-sell recommendations
